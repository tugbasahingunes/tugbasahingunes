# Turkish Style Guide: FitPlan

- **Address:** informal *sen* (fitness brand, young audience). Never mix *sen/siz* in one screen.
- **Tone:** encouraging and short. Use imperative for buttons (*Başla*, *Kaydet*), not infinitives (~~Başlamak~~).
- **Plurals:** Turkish nouns stay singular after numbers (*3 antrenman*, not ~~3 antrenmanlar~~), so both ICU branches use the singular.
- **Suffixes on variables:** avoid attaching suffixes to `{placeholders}` whose final vowel is unknown (*{workout}'u* ✗). Rephrase: *{workout} antrenmanını* ✓.
- **Apostrophes:** brand names take an apostrophe before suffixes: *FitPlan'de*, *Premium'un*.
- **Dates/numbers:** `{date}` is formatted by the app in tr-TR (*23 Kasım 2026*); decimal comma; currency after the amount (*99,99 TL*).
- **Length:** button text ≤ 120% of English length; push notifications ≤ 90 characters.
- **Capitalisation:** sentence case; Turkish casing for İ/ı (*İnternet*, not *Internet*).
