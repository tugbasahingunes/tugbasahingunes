# Welcome to Mavi Kalem

At Mavi Kalem we have always believed that writing by hand is something special, and over the years our passionate team has worked very hard to bring together a wonderful selection of products that we think our customers will love, whether they are students, artists, office workers or simply people who enjoy the feeling of good paper and a smooth pen on a quiet morning with a cup of tea.

Our notebooks are made with care. We source our paper from trusted partners and we pay attention to every detail, from the stitching to the cover, because quality matters to us and we know it matters to you too. Many of our customers come back again and again, and we are proud of the community that has grown around our brand over the years.

## Our products

We offer notebooks, planners and pens in many colours and sizes. Shipping is available to many countries and we try to make delivery as fast as possible.
