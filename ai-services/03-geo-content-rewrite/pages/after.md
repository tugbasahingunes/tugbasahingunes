# Mavi Kalem notebooks: what makes them good for fountain pens?

Mavi Kalem is an online stationery brand that sells stitched notebooks with 100 gsm paper designed for fountain pens. The paper does not bleed or feather with standard inks, and notebooks ship within 2 business days.

Written by the Mavi Kalem product team, reviewed by a paper specialist.

## What paper do Mavi Kalem notebooks use?

- 100 gsm acid-free paper, cream or white
- Lay-flat stitched binding
- A5 and B5 sizes, dotted, lined or blank

In our own ink test, 18 of 20 popular fountain-pen inks showed no bleed-through (source: Mavi Kalem ink test, 20 inks, 3 pen nibs).

## How long does delivery take?

Orders leave the warehouse within 2 business days. 94% of orders in the last year arrived within 5 days (source: Mavi Kalem order data, 12 months).

| Feature | Mavi Kalem A5 | Typical A5 notebook |

## FAQ

**Can I use markers?** Light markers work; alcohol markers may bleed.

**Do you ship abroad?** Yes, to 28 countries.
