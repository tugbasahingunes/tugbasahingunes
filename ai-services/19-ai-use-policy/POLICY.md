# Responsible AI Use Policy

*Template for a small or medium organisation. Replace items in [brackets].*

## 1. Purpose
This policy explains how staff at [Organisation] may use generative AI tools (such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Copilot) to work faster while protecting the people we serve, our data and our reputation.

## 2. Scope
It applies to all staff, volunteers and contractors who use AI tools for [Organisation] work, on any device.

## 3. Approved tools
| Tool | Approved for | Account type |
|---|---|---|
| [Tool A] | Drafting, summarising, translation drafts | Organisation account with data training turned off |
| [Tool B] | Coding and data analysis | Organisation account |

Personal accounts may be used only for tasks with no organisational or personal data.

## 4. Data rules (traffic light)
| Green: allowed | Amber: allowed only after removing names and identifiers | Red: never enter into an AI tool |
|---|---|---|
| Public information, our published materials, general questions | Internal reports, survey results, meeting notes | Personal data of clients or beneficiaries, health information, case files, passwords, financial account details, confidential contracts |

## 5. Human responsibility
- A named person is responsible for every AI-assisted output that leaves the organisation.
- Check facts, numbers, names and quotations against the original source.
- AI may draft; it may not make decisions about individuals (eligibility, hiring, risk assessments).

## 6. Transparency
- Tell readers when content is substantially AI-generated where it matters to them (for example, published research or reports to funders).
- Never present AI output as a quotation from a real person.

## 7. Fairness and quality
- Before using AI for any task that affects people, test it on examples from different groups and check for unequal treatment.
- Report errors or harmful outputs to [AI lead] using [channel].

## 8. Intellectual property
- Do not paste copyrighted material you are not allowed to share.
- Check that images and text produced by AI do not copy recognisable works or brands.

## 9. Training and review
- All staff complete a one-hour introduction before using approved tools.
- [AI lead] reviews this policy every 12 months, or sooner if the law or our tools change.

## 10. Breaches
Entering red-category data into an AI tool is a data incident and must be reported to [data protection contact] within 24 hours.
