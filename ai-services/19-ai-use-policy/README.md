# Responsible AI use policy for organisations

**Service:** a practical, one-to-two-page AI use policy your staff will actually read, tailored to your sector and tools, with a printable checklist and a short staff briefing.

**Demo:** [`POLICY.md`](POLICY.md), a ready-to-adapt template with 10 sections:

- Approved tools and account types
- A **traffic-light data rule** (green / amber / red), the part staff use most
- Human responsibility: AI may draft, it may not decide about individuals
- Transparency, fairness testing, intellectual property, review and breaches
- A 5-question checklist for daily use

Designed for NGOs, public bodies, schools, agencies and SMEs, especially organisations that handle personal or sensitive data.

`test_policy.py` checks that the template keeps all required sections when it is adapted for a client.

**Client deliverable:** a tailored policy (Word or Google Docs), a one-page staff summary, and an optional 45-minute online briefing.

> This is a governance template, not legal advice. For regulated sectors, the final text should be reviewed against local law (for example, data protection rules or the EU AI Act where relevant).
