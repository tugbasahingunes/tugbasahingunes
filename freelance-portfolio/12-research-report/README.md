# 12 · Research, Literature Review & Report Writing

**Client problem:** "I need a well-sourced background report / literature review / policy brief, written clearly."

**Sample:** [`policy_brief.md`](policy_brief.md): a 2-page policy brief on young people who are not in employment, education or training (NEET), my core research area (Ministry specialist thesis on NEET).

What a typical delivery includes:
- Clear research question and scope agreed in advance
- Structured findings with **every claim referenced** (APA 7)
- Executive summary for busy readers, recommendations, limitations
- Word/Google Docs/PDF in your template

Services: desk research, literature reviews, policy briefs, NGO/grant reports, thesis methodology support (editing and methods guidance, not ghost-writing), questionnaire design, report editing in Turkish and English.
