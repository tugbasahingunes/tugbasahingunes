# 10 · Translation & Localisation (Turkish ⇄ English)

**Client problem:** "We need our brochure / report / app text in natural English (or Turkish), not machine-translation English."

**Sample:** public-service information sheet, TR → EN (`source_tr.md` → `target_en.md`)

How I work:
1. **Glossary first** (`glossary.csv`): agreed terms, with UK/US notes
2. Human translation, with AI tools used for consistency checks only
3. **Automated QA** (`qa_check.py`): paragraph count, numbers, glossary terms
4. Native-style proofreading pass

Specialities: social policy, family & child services, public administration, academic papers and abstracts, NGO reports, app/website localisation, subtitles. English C1 (YDS 77.5), French B1.
