### Hi, I'm Tuğba 👋

I work where AI meets public services and social policy.

With 10+ years in social research, family services and data-driven policy, I build AI systems that hold up in regulated, high-stakes settings. That means turning how professionals actually work into clear criteria, keeping every output tied to evidence, and measuring when the system gets it wrong.

**How I work**

- **Start from the workflow.** I map how practitioners make decisions before deciding what AI should do.
- **Domain experts define "good".** Criteria and evaluation labels come from people who know the field, not from the model.
- **Evaluate what matters.** Different errors cost differently. A missed risk and a false accusation are not the same mistake.
- **Compliance by design.** EU AI Act, GDPR and human oversight are built in from the start, not added at the end.

**Areas I know well**

Social protection and care services · family and child policy · youth employment (NEET) · digital child protection · official statistics and survey research · public-sector data
