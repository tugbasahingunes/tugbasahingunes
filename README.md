### Hi, I'm Tuğba 👋

I work where data, AI and social policy meet.

With 10+ years in social research, family services and data-driven policy, I build AI systems that hold up in regulated, high-stakes settings. That means turning how professionals actually work into clear criteria, keeping every output tied to evidence, and measuring when the system gets it wrong.

**How I work**

- **Start from the workflow.** I map how practitioners make decisions before deciding what AI should do.
- **Domain experts define "good".** Criteria and evaluation labels come from people who know the field, not from the model.
- **Evaluate what matters.** Different errors cost differently. A missed risk and a false accusation are not the same mistake.
- **Compliance by design.** EU AI Act, GDPR and human oversight are built in from the start, not added at the end.

**Areas I know well**

Social protection and care services · family and child policy · youth employment (NEET) · digital child protection · official statistics and survey research · public-sector data

**Selected work**

📊 [**Social Policy Data Lab**](https://github.com/tugbasahingunes/social-policy-data-lab): 13 reproducible analyses with open international data (Eurostat, European Social Survey, ILO). Youth NEET and its gender gap, who works in the jobs most exposed to generative AI, why Finnish adults are among Europe's most active AI users, what makes Europeans happy, a fairness audit of a machine-learning early-warning model, youth unemployment forecasts, and whether Germany's minimum wage cost young people jobs. Methods include weighted logistic and multilevel regression, Oaxaca–Blinder decomposition, dominance analysis, multiple imputation, gradient boosting with SHAP, time-series forecasting with conformal intervals, synthetic control and synthetic difference-in-differences, and bootstrap inference, each with a methodology note.

🛡️ [**When a child tells a chatbot**](https://github.com/tugbasahingunes/vulnerable-user-ai-test): a safety evaluation for vulnerable young users. 547 tests in English and Turkish built on the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child, scoring what a good answer must *contain* when a child discloses a risk, and penalising over-reaction to ordinary teenage messages. Showcase repository; full set available on request.

📋 [**CaseNote AI**](https://github.com/tugbasahingunes/casenote-ai): AI support for social care home-visit notes, with expert-defined criteria, evidence quotes for every flag, coverage-gap reporting and an evaluation that separates *missed signals* from *harmful over-flagging*. Criteria live in a separate file, so the same pipeline can serve another country, service or client group.
